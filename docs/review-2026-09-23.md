## Checks that came out clean

   - for every `d ≥ 4`, every graph on `Fin n` (any `n`, including 0) with strictly fewer than `C(d+2,2)` edges has an injective placement in ℝᵈ with every edge at length 1;
   - `K_{d+2}` has exactly `C(d+2,2)` edges;
   - `K_{d+2}` has no such placement.

   This is `f(d) = C(d+2,2)`, with no off-by-one error and nothing vacuous. The witness is `K₆` at `d = 4` together with the claim itself. `drop1` is proved at `d = 0`. `Palomar.target` is `edgeThreshold` with no extra hypothesis in both `Challenge.lean` and `Solution.lean`.
